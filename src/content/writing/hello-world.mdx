Most of what I make never gets shown. Half-finished extensions, scripts I wrote
once and never opened again, a folder called `experiments/` that's mostly dead
ends. This site is the opposite of that — it's the small set of things I
actually want to point at.

I'll write here when I figure something out the hard way, or when I have an
opinion strong enough to defend. No schedule. No newsletter. Just the work.
